Walk through the customer app, the pro app, the admin panel and the website. Everything here is sample data. Nothing is sent or saved.

Customer app

The customer signs in with Apple or Google, confirms the address on a map and sends a request. They never choose or vet a contractor. Tap through the phone, or jump to a screen.

  1. Welcome and sign in
  2. Your details
  3. Allow location
  4. Address on the map
  5. Home
  6. Request a service
  7. Finding a pro
  8. Order status and payment

Pro app

Contractors cannot sign up by themselves. Every request goes to all vetted pros nearby, and the first to accept gets the job, with a route in Google Maps or Apple Maps.

  1. Sign in
  2. New job, first to accept
  3. Accepted job and route
  4. Jobs and earnings
